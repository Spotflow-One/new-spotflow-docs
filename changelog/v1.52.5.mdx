## New Features

- Public/Secret Key Security Model: This is a big one. We've rolled out a major security enhancement by separating key functions. Your Public Key is now used for client-side communications (like checkout), while your Secret Key stays on your server for server-to-server communications. This separation of duties is a massive leap forward for platform security.

## Improvements & Updates

- Checkout Expiry Control: You can now set an expiry time for checkout URLs. If a customer doesn't complete the purchase in time, the session is logged with a `session_expired` status, giving you much better control over abandoned carts and time-sensitive offers.
- Reliable Webhooks for Accounts & Wallets: You'll now always receive a webhook from Spotflow whenever a credit or debit happens on the accounts and wallets you've created for your customers.
- Payment Link UI Tidy-up: We tidied up the Payment Link creation and management interface on both desktop and mobile. It's just cleaner, faster, and nicer to use.
- The Usual Housekeeping: A healthy round of performance optimizations, security tweaks, and minor bug fixes to keep the entire platform running smoothly.
