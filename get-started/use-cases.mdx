|---|---|---|
| Sell a product or service and take one-time payments | Payment collections | [Accept payments](/features/payment-collections) · [Quickstart](/quickstart) |
| Take payments without writing code | Payment links | [Payment links in under 5 minutes](/developer-resources/integration-guide) |
| Charge customers on a recurring schedule | Subscriptions | [Subscription billing](/features/subscription-payment-collections) · [Bill a customer every month](/developer-resources/guides/bill-a-customer-every-month) |
| Bill customers who pay with mobile money | Mobile money subscriptions | [Mobile money subscriptions](/features/mobile-money-subscriptions) |
| Offer free trials or discounts on a plan | Plan trials and currency rules | [Plan trials and currency rules](/features/plan-trials-and-currency-rules) |
| Give each customer an account number to pay into | Virtual accounts | [Give a customer a virtual account](/developer-resources/guides/give-a-customer-a-virtual-account) |
| Keep funds separate for different customers or purposes | Static virtual accounts | [Static accounts](/features/static-accounts) |
| Route incoming transfers straight to your main balance | Dynamic virtual accounts | [Dynamic accounts](/features/dynamic-accounts) |
| Pay a supplier, contractor or customer | Single disbursements | [Pay one person out](/developer-resources/guides/pay-one-person-out) |
| Pay many people at once, such as payroll | Bulk disbursements | [Pay many people at once](/developer-resources/guides/pay-many-people-at-once) |
| Collect VAT or a service fee | Tax configuration | [Charge tax on a collection](/developer-resources/guides/charge-tax-on-a-collection) |
| Get your revenue into your bank account | Settlement | [Settlement](/features/settlement) · [Payout structure](/features/payout-structure) |
